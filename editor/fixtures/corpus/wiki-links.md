# Links with backslashes

A backslash in a link is text: [[Plan\]], \[[Escaped]], [[A\|B]], [[Plan\#Soil]] and [[a\\b]].

A name Inkkit does not read as a link stays as written: [[a]b]], [[a[b]], [[id|A|B]] and [[a|b|c]].

[[Plan#Heading|an alias]] and [[22222222-2222-4222-8222-222222222222|A#B]] are ordinary links.
