# Rejected designs

Status: Reference. Each design below was drawn and turned down.

Where it lives: this page records choices that have no code to show for them. The pages it links to describe what MAPP does instead.

Do not try one of these again in the same form. If a new case needs one, say what is different and ask first.

## Chips and values

- **A type in a neutral chip with a gray edge.** A tint means a type; see [Value components](value-components.md).
- **A tag drawn with the tag symbol or an outline.** A tag is gray, after a number sign.
- **A person in a gray chip.** A person is initials in a pale circle, with no chip and no edge.
- **Glass type marks with a drop shadow.**
- **A chip taller than its neighbors.** Chips side by side must line up, so every chip is one height.
- **Chips squeezed to a few letters.** Fit whole chips, then show "+N".
- **A person drawn smaller than the chips beside it.**
- **A bordered plus button for an empty value.** An empty value is the gray word Empty.
- **Bare chips.**
- **Pen-style highlights.** Highlights are yellow, with other colors as settings.

## The item page

- **Pins each in a box with a fill, or as one strip with hairlines.**
- **Larger text in pins.**
- **A placeholder for a pin that has no value**, such as a dotted place or a "drop a pin" box, in Customize Layout or on the page.
- **Customize Layout from a menu on each row**, or Customize Page in a sheet. It is the right sidebar. See [Item page](item-page.md).
- **A "when" band** above the title or between the properties, and a line of text between the title and the pins.
- **Accent rails on callouts**, and any side rail except on a quote block.
- **Dividers that run to the edges of a grouped box.**
- **Centered headers.**
- **Blue links for maps and places.**
- **Empty frames where a picture belongs.**

## Rows, cards and views

- **A status and a type's mark sharing one leading column of a row.**
- **A type shown twice in one row**, as a mark and as a chip.
- **Blank space hanging under a row's leading mark.**
- **A type tile at the trailing edge of a row, or a large leading type tile.**
- **A person's name in a row.** A row shows initials only.
- **A clipboard item titled with its content.** It is titled "Clipboard Item", or a name its person gave it.
- **A gray key, such as a number, before a title.** A number is a property.
- **Sources as cards with a type breakdown bar.**
- **Gallery text centered under the card, or over the picture.**
- **Gallery cards too wide or too tall, and pictures at landscape height.**
- **A board without its columns.**
- **An agenda narrowed to a column.** The timeline uses the window's width.
- **A thumbnail column of a new kind beside a document.**
- **A flat, squared or hand-drawn segmented control.** MAPP uses the system's. See [Views and lists](views-and-lists.md).
- **A separate filter button in the list toolbar.** Filters live in the row above the list.

## Buttons and messages

- **Capsule buttons, black buttons and the Mac's push buttons.** Every button MAPP draws is a rounded rectangle; see [Agreed designs](agreed-designs.md).
- **A large 36-point button size.** There are two sizes, 22 and 28 points.
- **A blue focus ring.** Keyboard focus is a gray ring.
- **A solid red destructive button.** It is a pale red fill with red text.
- **MAPP's own alert.** MAPP uses Apple's.
- **A toast at the top of the page.** It rises at the bottom of the window.

## Selection

- **Blue for a chosen row, card or table row.** It is a soft gray.
- **A gray plate behind a chosen gallery card.** The card keeps a soft ring.

## The sidebar and Settings

- **A tab bar when there is only one tab.**
- **A Customize Sidebar button in the sidebar.** Customize from a Control-click or in Settings > Sidebar.
- **Customizing the sidebar in a window of its own, or in a panel beside a section.**
- **Checkboxes and a dashed Show More line while customizing.** A section's menu sets how many show.
- **Handles at the leading edge, beside the marks.** They sit at each row's trailing edge.
- **A view's mark as a round tinted tile.** It is the view's symbol in its own color.
- **Settings taking over the window's sidebar**, with a way back and a search field. Settings keeps the app's sidebar and its list.
- **Descriptions of three lines or more in Settings.** One line, two at most.

## Collections and Sources

- **Collections as a gallery**, for now. They are a table.
- **Buttons in the right sidebar**, such as Rename, Change Mark and Show Items. The right sidebar can be narrow.
- **A back button from an open collection**, for now.
- **The bottom-right corner as a strip along the page's foot.**

## Status

- **Drawn status rings.** Status uses the app's SF Symbols.

## Palettes

- **An Actions panel floating above the palette's bar.** Actions takes the bar's place. See [Views and lists](views-and-lists.md).
- **A filter button in the command palette.** Only Search Items has one.
- **A wide pill for the palette's filter button.** It is as wide as it is tall.
- **Showing the palette's filters by making the palette taller.** The row takes its room from the list.
- **Rows cut off hard at the palette's bar.** Rows scroll under the bar and fade out with the system's soft scroll edge effect.
- **Filled key caps in the palette's buttons.** Key caps are outlined, with no fill.
- **Open and Actions as two separate capsules.** They are one capsule with two segments.
- **Open at Match as the palette's button label.** It reads Open, and Search Items still opens at the match.
- **An accent-colored filter button when filters are set.** It stays neutral and shows a soft fill.
- **A filter row's + larger than its chips.** It is as tall as a chip.
- **One-line and two-line rows in one palette list.** Every row in a list is one height.
- **More than three palette materials, or a Tuning knob for them.** Settings offers Glass, Translucent and Solid.
- **Actions morphing out of the bar's glass.** A Liquid Glass morph around Actions' field hung the app when Actions opened; it grows with an ordinary transition.
- **A divider under the palette's field.**
- **Large type marks in Search Items rows.** Its rows use the command palette's small mark.
