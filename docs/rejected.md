# Rejected designs

Search this page before you propose a look. Each line is a refused design, then what takes its place. If a case needs one, say what is different and ask. Some are still on `main`; a row in `docs/agreed.md` removes them.

## Words and the model

- **An Inbox as a place.** The Feed, and a view a person makes.
- **"From Apps" or "Yours".** Feed and Library.
- **A type that opens on the Feed by rule.** All Items, and the setting.
- **Words of waiting** ("waits", "until", a count to clear) for the Feed.
- **Library > Tags**, as a row or a list. A tag's own list, from its chip.
- **A Home page, or a filter that leaves types out of every place.**

## Chips, marks and values

- **A tinted chip, except for the type being given to an item,** or a hairline round a status. A type or a link is a mark and a name, and every other chip is the one quiet wash.
- **A glass mark with a shadow, or a filled tile behind a sidebar icon.** The plain mark.
- **A person in a gray chip, in gray or with an edge, or drawn smaller than the chips beside.** Initials in a pale purple circle with no edge.
- **A tag with the tag symbol or an outline.** The `number` symbol, on the one chip wash.
- **Chips of two heights, or squeezed to a few letters.** Fit whole chips, then a count.
- **A bordered plus for an empty value.** The word Empty.
- **A heart, or drawn status rings.** The star, and the app's symbols.

## Item page

- **Pins in boxes, in a strip or in larger text, or properties in two columns.** Rows.
- **A "when" band for an event.** A row.
- **A person's photograph as a hero.** The header every type has.
- **A placeholder for a value that a pinned row lacks.** Empty.
- **A side rail except on a quote**, a centered header, or blue links for maps.
- **An empty frame where a picture belongs.** One quiet line with Load, where the picture is.
- **Formats as a card or a menu of styles.** One bar at the page's foot.
- **Text size inside the formats.** One control with a menu.
- **A panel as a rail, quiet cards, or labels and lines.** One panel with pills.
- **Playback controls on the picture or under the title.** A bar at the foot.
- **Row numbers on by default in a table item**, or a table item with properties and margins. The sheet alone.

## Rows, cards and views

- **Filled gray cards for sections**, or a stack of rows above a list. Plain headings.
- **A type shown twice in a row, or a status sharing the mark's column.**
- **A type tile at a row's trailing edge, or a large mark before a row or a title.** The small mark.
- **A person's name in a row.** Initials.
- **A key or number before a title.** A property.
- **A clipboard item titled with its content.** "Clipboard Item", or its given name.
- **Gallery text centered or over the picture**, or cards at landscape height.
- **A timeline in a narrow column.** The window's width.
- **A filter button in the toolbar, or a filter row that always shows.** The View panel.
- **Save as View in the search field or the toolbar.** The plus, which starts from the search.
- **The place's control at the end of the row of views.** The control after the title.
- **A week number on the week layout, or a header of two lines.** One line.

## Making an item

- **The kinds as tiles in the new item line.** Rows with keys.
- **A card in the screen's corner for a new item from any app.** The same panel in the upper third.
- **A glass New Item button in no results.** Flat words on the field's line.
- **A New row on Collections or Sources.** The plus.

## Selection

- **Blue for a chosen row, card or cell.** Soft gray.
- **A gray plate behind a chosen card.** A soft ring.
- **A selection bar whose count is a token beside plain words.** The capsule of symbols.
- **A toolbar, a strip of words or the place of the column names** for what acts on several chosen items. The capsule.

## Buttons and messages

- **Black buttons, or one shape at every size.** The shape follows the size.
- **A button in a Settings section's header.** Adding is the card's last row.
- **A row's action only on the row under the pointer in Settings.** Show it on every row.
- **A sheet or alert under the toolbar.** The middle of the window.
- **A blue focus ring or a solid red button.** A gray ring, and pale red with red text.
- **An alert of the app's own.** The system's.
- **A toast at the top, or one with Close.** The foot, with Undo.

## Sidebar

- **The Marfa's name at the top, an app mark beside it, or a green dot with "Up to date".** The line at the foot.
- **An "Add a Marfa…" row in the Marfa's panel.** The plus on its heading.
- **A tab bar with one tab, or a Customize button in the sidebar.** A Control-click.
- **Customizing in Settings, a window or a panel.** The sidebar in place, handles trailing.
- **Checkboxes or a Show More line while customizing.** A section's menu sets how many show.
- **A view's mark as a round tile, or pins with indentation.** Its symbol.
- **An In Sidebar column in Types.** The row's menu.

## Settings and the first run

- **Settings in the main window or with drawn controls.** Its own window and the system's controls.
- **Three lines under a title.** Two at most.
- **Gray cards, or a page split by segments.** White cards with a hairline.
- **Back beside a page's title.** The toolbar.
- **Minus and plus for a number, or one text field for a list of words.** A short field or a menu; edit each line in place.
- **A Paper theme, a Rows and Cards page, or Advanced as a page.**
- **Setup inside the main window with the sidebar waiting** (the onboarding experiment).

## Toolbar, corner and opening

- **Back and Forward in the page's toolbar, or a labeled Back.** Over the sidebar.
- **The panel's button inside the page.** The far end of the toolbar.
- **Close in one group with Up and Down in the modal.** Its own button.
- **The corner in the modal, as a strip, or in the accent.** Separate buttons, gray when on.
- **An item sliding over the list (a side peek).** The modal, the page or a window.
- **Zoom on a picture or a PDF as separate buttons, or "Page 3 of 12".** One control with a menu, and "3 of 12".
- **Calendar's controls in the toolbar or one bar.** Separate corner buttons.
- **Focus that hides the sidebar, or Full Screen as its name.** Focus hides the title and rows.
- **Buttons in the panel**, such as Rename. The panel can be narrow.

## Palettes

- **Actions floating above the bar, or morphing out of glass.** It takes the bar's place.
- **A filter button in the command palette.** Only Search Items has one, neutral.
- **A taller palette for filters, rows of two heights, or large marks.** Keep one shape.
- **A divider under the field, hard-cut rows, or filled keycaps.** Rows fade at the bar.
- **Open and Actions as two capsules, or "Open at Match".** One capsule, and Open.
- **A fourth material.**
