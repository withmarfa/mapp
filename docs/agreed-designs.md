## Settings

Agreed with August in session 171k (19 and 20 November 2026). The pages themselves are drawn on the Settings tab of MAPP Pages and the design system holds every part (`SettingsWindow` and the components it uses); what each page holds is for his review, so it is not listed here yet.

- **A window of its own.** Settings opens in its own window, not as a place in the main window's sidebar, so the Marfa's pages can one day stand alone as their own app. One sidebar lists MAPP's pages, then the Marfa's, in groups split by space, each page with a colour tile, and a search field at the top. The content is a column at most 620 points wide, with more room either side as the window widens.
- **The toolbar carries the page's name** beside Back and Forward. There is no back button beside a title.
- **Rows.** A row is a lead, a title with one line under it (two at most), and one trailing edge. Rows are 44 points, 56 with a line, 36 for a list of words. Every input is 200 by 28 and ends on the same line. Leads sit in one 24-point column, so text starts at 52 and the divider at the text.
- **Sections.** Every section is named; a thing's head card is the one exception. Cards are white with a hairline, never gray.
- **Changing things.** A list of words or patterns is changed in place (double-click or Return edits a line; Add a Name or Pattern adds one; order is shown where it matters). Something new with several fields is a sheet, in the middle of the window. Apple's alert asks before what can't be undone. A thing with settings of its own is a page.
- **Numbers.** A field with its unit inside, or a pop-up of sensible steps, then Custom, where steps guide the person.
- **Problems and errors.** A problem row offers only actions that exist: Ignore and Edit… where both do, one button and a menu where there are more. An error uses the design system's red: a ring and one line under the title, never more.
- **Buttons are gray**, the confirm button too; blue only for Update. Focus is a gray ring.
- **Shortcuts.** Keyboard lists every command in a white shortcut field with a clear button, with a small search at the top right of the list and Restore Defaults… at the end.
- **Tables.** The filter on the left and a small search on the right, above the table. In a section, a light table with no rules between rows, a page at a time; a table that is a page of its own fills the pane, with columns, selection and actions at the foot.
- **Words.** "Permanently Delete", never "for good".
- **A synced folder's page** shows what it holds as a tree of its folders, counted in files, which MAPP builds from the server's paths.
